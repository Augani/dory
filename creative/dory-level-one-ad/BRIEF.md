## Intent

Create a fun, premium, high-energy video ad for Dory to post on X. The competitive
idea is breadth rather than an unsupported speed claim: a local runtime that stops at
containers reaches only level one, while Dory continues through the rest of the local
Linux development stack.

The approved style is a tactile modern arcade world built from exact Dory colors and
brand assets. It should feel playful enough to stop the feed and precise enough to
earn a developer's trust. The story must remain completely understandable with sound
off.

## Notes

- Do not use competitor logos or imply that every competing product lacks every Dory capability.
- Do not claim Dory is faster, lighter overall, first, or only; comparative performance is not yet qualified.
- Use no purple cyberpunk gradients, generic particle fields, tiny dashboard copy, or pixel-art styling.
- Preserve the canonical Dory mark exactly; animate only its outer wrapper.
- Product boundary: Apple Silicon Mac and macOS 14 or later.

## X export target

- 1080×1080 square, 30 fps, 21.0 seconds.
- MP4 with H.264 High profile, 4:2:0 pixel format, AAC-LC stereo, and fast-start metadata.
- Target roughly 6–10 Mbps while keeping the upload comfortably below 30 MB.
- The opening must read immediately without audio; the final CTA must hold at least one second.
