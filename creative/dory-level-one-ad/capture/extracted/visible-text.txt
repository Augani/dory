Product truth:

Dory includes its own Docker 29 engine and CLI, Buildx, BuildKit, Compose v2,
one-click local k3s, full Debian 13 / Ubuntu 24.04 LTS / Kali rolling Xfce
desktops, persistent headless Linux machines, transactional migration and
recovery, and policy-enforced dedicated agent sandbox VMs. It is a native Mac
app, GPL-3.0, free and open source, with no Dory account or telemetry.
